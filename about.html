AI models

Strongman AI uses tuned assistants for workout generation, coaching tone, and safety guardrails. Details may change as we ship updates.

EDDIE

Primary coaching model · v1.1

Named for the sport’s legends, EDDIE focuses on structured programming, progressive overload, and plain-language explanations. It is not a substitute for medical advice—consult a professional for injuries or health conditions.

  • Workout generation from natural-language prompts
  • Session summaries and progressive adjustments (when enabled)
  • Safety-oriented refusal on unsafe or ambiguous requests
