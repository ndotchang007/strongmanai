Overview

Strongman AI is an AI gym app made for all gym users. We understand that, for beginners, starting to workout can be intimidating and, for advanced, it's possible to lose motivation to workout. We at Strongman AI aim to solve both these issues by making our apps tailored to easy-use and accurate statistics. We've also added features for users to show off their own progress and motivate others through a social media page. To increase the enjoyment of working out, we also added a competition page to allow users to compete with their friends.
