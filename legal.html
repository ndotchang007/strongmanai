AI coaching (Claude)

Rocky and other AI features in Strongman AI are powered by Anthropic’s Claude models through our backend API. We operate the API credentials — you do not need to supply your own key to use Rocky.

When we call Claude on your behalf

Content you submit to Rocky (chat messages, athlete profile context, and related prompts) may be transmitted to Anthropic’s services to produce a response. Anthropic’s terms, usage policies, and privacy documentation apply to that processing in addition to ours. You can review Anthropic’s policies at anthropic.com/legal/consumer-terms and anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Do not submit secrets, full payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive personal data into AI fields unless you understand the risks.

Workouts Rocky builds are suggestions only. You choose whether to apply them to your logbook or follow them in training.

Chat history & local storage

Saved on your device

  • Browser storage. Your Rocky conversation may be saved in your browser’s local storage so you can continue where you left off. It stays on your device until you clear chat or clear site data.
  • Encrypted in transit. When you send a message, it travels to our servers over HTTPS/TLS. Our servers forward the necessary context to Claude for that request.
  • No permanent server archive. We use recent messages in a request to maintain conversational context, but we do not store your full chat history as a long-term server-side log.
  • Shared devices. If you use a shared or public computer, clear chat or log out when finished so others cannot read your conversation.

Usage limits

Rocky messages may be subject to daily usage limits to control cost and abuse. Limits are shown in the coach interface when applicable.
