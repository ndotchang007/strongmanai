                        Strength overview
                        Reading your logs…
                        Tap to hide / show
                    
                    

                        

                            These charts show whether you’re getting stronger — rising load and estimated maxes mean progress; flat lines mean you’re maintaining.
                        

                        
    
                        
    
                            
    Training volume — total weight × reps across a session. Upward trend usually means more work capacity / strength.
    
                            
    Peak load — heaviest working set for a lift. Climbing peaks are the clearest “gaining strength” signal.
    
                            
    Est. 1RM — estimated max from your best set (Epley). Useful when you don’t test true singles.
    
                            
    Session frequency — how often you train. Strength needs both load and enough sessions.
    
                            
    Intensity score — how hard sessions felt (your 0–100 rating), not the same as bar weight.
