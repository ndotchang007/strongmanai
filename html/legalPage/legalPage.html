OpenAI & API keys

Strongman AI may use OpenAI (or other model APIs) to power coaching text, workout generation, or similar features. This section clarifies how that relates to you—especially if you provide your own API key.

When we call OpenAI on your behalf

Content you submit to AI features may be transmitted to OpenAI’s services to produce a response. OpenAI’s terms, usage policies, and privacy documentation apply to that processing in addition to ours. You can review OpenAI’s policies at openai.com/policies/terms-of-use and openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Do not submit secrets, full payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or other highly sensitive personal data into AI fields unless you understand the risks.

If you supply your own OpenAI API key

Bring-your-own-key (BYOK) features

  • Your account with OpenAI. Your key is tied to your OpenAI billing and usage limits. You are responsible for all charges and for complying with OpenAI’s terms and acceptable use rules.
  • Security. Treat your API key like a password. Do not paste it into public forums, screenshots, or source control. Anyone with the key can incur usage on your account.
  • How the key may be used. If the product lets you save a key, it may be stored or transmitted so our servers (or your browser, depending on implementation) can call OpenAI when you request AI features. Only enter a key if you accept that risk profile.
  • Revocation. You can rotate or revoke your key in the OpenAI dashboard at any time. Remove or replace the key in Strongman AI settings if you no longer want the Service to use it.
  • No warranty from OpenAI. Strongman AI is not responsible for OpenAI’s availability, pricing, model behavior, or policy changes.

If a feature does not ask for an API key, AI requests may use infrastructure and credentials operated by Strongman AI instead. In that case, your inputs are still sent to the model provider as needed to generate responses, under our privacy practices above.
